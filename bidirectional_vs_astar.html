When this matters: Road networks (millions of nodes), uniform branching graphs, and long-distance routing (Paris to Berlin). On small graphs (like 6 nodes), the overhead of managing two open sets outweighs the savings. The real power shows at scale: with branching factor k and depth d, A* explores k^d nodes while Bidirectional explores just 2 x k^(d/2) — an exponential reduction.
